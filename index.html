The part every other app skips

The app that actually corrects your tones

Tones are the hardest part of Taiwanese Hokkien. Other apps play a recording and hope you can hear the difference. Bobamama listens to how you say it and tells you which tone went wrong and how to fix it.

  • Say a word out loud, get instant tone-by-tone feedback
  • Practice tone pairs and sandhi with targeted drills
  • Track which tones you keep missing, then drill them until they stick

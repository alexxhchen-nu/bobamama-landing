What this covers

Bobamama is a Taiwanese Hokkien learning product and its marketing site (the “shop”). This draft describes how we intend to handle information when the product is live.

What we collect today

The current landing waitlist is a prototype: the form does not store your email on a server yet. Anything you type stays in your browser for that demo only.

Hosting and analytics providers for this static site may log standard technical data (for example IP address, user agent, and pages requested). We do not control those logs beyond what the host offers.

What we may collect later

  • Account or waitlist email, if you choose to join
  • Learning progress and preference settings inside the app
  • Speech or audio you submit for tone practice, if that feature ships with recording
  • Support messages you send us

We will update this page before those features store personal data.

How we use information

  • To run the shop and learning product
  • To email early-access updates you asked for
  • To improve tone practice and product quality
  • To keep the service secure and working

We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We may use processors (hosting, email, analytics, speech tooling) that only handle data to provide their service to us. We will name those processors here when they are chosen.

Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Waitlist and account deletion requests: [contact email].

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact [contact email].

Children

Bobamama is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). If you believe a child gave us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
